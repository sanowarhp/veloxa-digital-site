Case study

Giftoria — premium gift bundles, built to actually convert.

A men's gift-bundle e-commerce store selling via Cash on Delivery in Bangladesh. Every part of the site — storefront, checkout, and ad tracking — was built and is maintained in-house.

The brief

Turn a single-product idea into a full storefront that could handle browsing, bundle customization, and a Cash-on-Delivery checkout — without losing customers to a clunky process on mobile.

What was built

A full storefront, not a landing page.

Checkout

Multi-step order wizard

Bundle, edition, and size selection flowing into customer info and an order summary — built to reduce drop-off on mobile.

Catalog

Product & bundle pages

Image galleries, edition selectors, quantity steppers, and related-product suggestions.

Trust

Reviews & social proof

Video testimonials, real customer chat screenshots, and Facebook recommendations, with privacy masking applied.

Tracking

Meta Pixel + CAPI

Fixed a duplicate tracking tag that was silently breaking purchase data, then improved match quality with hashed identifiers.

Speed

WebP & caching

Images compressed to WebP; LiteSpeed, Cloudflare, and OPcache caching tuned for consistent load times.

Security

Admin hardening

Brute-force login protection added to the admin panel.
